Heritage & Purity

This isn't just olive oil; it's a liquid legacy. Sourced exclusively from the century-old groves of Valle Verde, each olive is hand-plucked at the dawn of peak ripeness to ensure a profile that is both robust and elegantly nuanced.

Our cold-pressing technique follows a ancestral heritage method, where the fruit is crushed between granite stones within four hours of harvest. This prevents oxidation and preserves the volatile aromatic compounds that give our oil its signature peppery finish and notes of freshly cut grass and green almond.
